About

Film Editor · Post Producer · Post Supervisor — and director. 11+ years.

Art. Emotion.
Rhythm.

I began my artistic journey at fourteen as a theater actor, then moved into directing and won awards for my short films. I went on to work as a visual effects artist before leading post-production — so I understand every part of how a film is made.

That background shaped how I cut: emotion, music and performance first. Today I lead post from the offline edit to the final master for features, TV series, documentaries and campaigns — for MBC Shahid, the Saudi Ministry of Culture, Riyad Bank, Half Million and more.
